## What it says

Patanjali's Yoga Sutras lay out yoga as eight limbs — ashtanga — arranged as a
concentric progression from outer conduct to inner absorption:

1. **Yama** — restraints in conduct toward others
2. **Niyama** — observances in conduct toward oneself
3. **Asana** — steady, comfortable posture
4. **Pranayama** — regulation of breath
5. **Pratyahara** — withdrawal of the senses
6. **Dharana** — concentration on a single point
7. **Dhyana** — sustained meditation
8. **Samadhi** — absorption

The order is the argument. The limbs move from the most external — how one
treats other people — inward through the body and breath, then past the senses
into attention itself. Each ring steadies the next. The progression does not
ask for belief; it describes a training sequence, and the interior limbs are
simply unreachable while the outer ones are in disorder.
