_Written on 08.08.2026._

1. I am deeply connected with myself, the Divine and my spiritual path. I live
   with awareness, gratitude, love and inner peace.

2. I am in the best health of my life. My body is my temple, and I am strong,
   disciplined, energetic, clean and healthy.

3. I am giving my love, time and presence to Mom, Dad, Naina, my family and the
   beautiful souls in my life, and creating wonderful memories with them.

4. I am an inspiring builder and leader, creating revolutionary things with
   love that improve human life and make this planet better.

5. I am expressing myself authentically and confidently, sharing my ideas with
   the world and connecting with extraordinary people and minds.

6. I am receiving extraordinary abundance, wealth, opportunities, businesses,
   investments and success through the value I create.

7. I am living a life of freedom, love, adventure, meaningful relationships,
   beautiful experiences and work that gives me purpose.

8. I am trusting the universe and giving my whole heart to this journey. I take
   inspired action, let go of fear and attachment, surrender, and receive with
   gratitude.

> The universe is perfect.<br />
> Everything is connected.<br />
> My body is my temple.<br />
> My vision is to inspire.
